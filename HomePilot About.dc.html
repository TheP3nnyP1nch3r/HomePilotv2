The listing that started it.

A seller came to us after her home had sat for four months. She'd done everything her agent asked. The price was wrong from day one and nobody had told her, because the conversation that would have fixed it was uncomfortable and the commission got paid either way.

We repriced it, rewrote the positioning, and coached her through two offers in eleven days. Then we ran the numbers on what that advice would have cost her at 3%. It was more than her kitchen renovation.

That was the whole idea. Charge for the strategy, not for the size of someone's house.

So we built the FlightPlan — the process we'd already been running informally — and put one price on it. Sellers keep the difference. We keep the part of the job that actually requires a license and a spine.
